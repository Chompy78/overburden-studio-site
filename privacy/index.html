Mine Life privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Who we are

Mine Life ("the game") is published by Overburden Studio, Western Australia. Contact for privacy questions: overburdenstudio@gmail.com.

Short version

The game works fully offline. It does not collect, send or share any information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What stays on your phone

Your settings, game progress, saved games, scores, the names you give your manager, company and mines, and simple play counts (for example how many projects you have played) are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. They are removed when you uninstall the game or clear its storage.

What the game does not do

The game does not connect to the internet by itself. It does not read your name, email, contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera. It does not use your advertising ID or any other device identifier. The only Android permission it asks for is vibration.

Paid add-ons

If the game offers paid add-ons, you buy them through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. Google tells the game which add-ons you own so it can unlock them, and Google gives us its standard sales records (such as the order number, product, date, price and country). We use those records only for accounting and support, as Google Play's terms allow. Google's own privacy policy covers the purchase.

Optional: sending a problem report

Settings has two buttons, "Send diagnostics" and "Report a problem". Only if you tap one does the game make a file on your device and open your phone's share menu. You decide whether to send it, with which app (for example email) and to whom. The game never sends it on its own.

The file contains: the game version; your device model, Android version, screen size and graphics chip; your game settings; your campaign progress (without your player name); the last part of the game's log; your current or last save; frame-rate figures; recent game events; and your own description of the problem if you write one. It does not contain your name, accounts, device IDs, location or contacts. The same buttons can also save the file in your Downloads folder, in a folder called MineLife.

If you email the file to us, we receive it together with your email address. We use it only to find and fix problems, we do not share or sell it, and we delete it once the problem is dealt with, or within 12 months, or sooner if you ask. Your email provider's own policy covers how the message travels. Please do not send us a report if you are under 13.

This page

The game opens this page in your web browser when you tap "Privacy policy" in the game. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages; your browser and GitHub may record the visit under their own policies (GitHub's privacy statement).

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Google Play, which you use to download the game and buy any add-ons, has its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The game is made for people aged 13 and over. It collects nothing by itself, so nothing is collected from anyone of any age. We do not knowingly receive personal information from children under 13; if we find we have, we delete it.

Your rights

We hold no information about you unless you emailed us a report or a message, or bought an add-on (in which case we hold Google's sales record). To see, correct or delete what we hold, write to overburdenstudio@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. People in Australia, the European Union and the United Kingdom have legal rights over their data, and you may also complain to your local privacy regulator.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version replaces this one on this page with a new effective date. If the game ever starts collecting more data, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form will be updated before that version is released.
